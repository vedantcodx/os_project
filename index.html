Where each OS concept appears

  • Process and thread: arrival generator, dispatcher, doctors, fault monitor and metrics are separate logical threads in the monitor.
  • Producer-consumer: the generator adds patients to the queues, doctor threads take them out and sleep on a condition variable when the queues are empty.
  • Mutual exclusion: queue changes and patient assignment run under one mutex; the lock operation count is shown in the thread monitor.
  • Semaphores: ICU beds, ventilators and the X-ray unit are counting semaphores with FIFO waiters.
  • Deadlock avoidance: every doctor requests resources in the same fixed order (ICU bed, ventilator, X-ray).
  • Priority scheduling and aging: the policy selector and the α, β, γ sliders; dashed outlines mark patients past their wait threshold.
  • Fault handling: a missed heartbeat releases the doctor's resources and returns the patient to the queue.

Educational simulation with synthetic patients. Triage levels, treatment times and weights are simplified and are not clinical guidance. JavaScript runs on one thread, so the threads here are logical threads scheduled on a virtual clock (one tick equals one simulated minute). The C++ design with std::thread, std::mutex and std::condition_variable is described in the project report.
